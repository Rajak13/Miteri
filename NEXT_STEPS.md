## 🆘 Common Issues & Solutions

### "My site isn't showing in Google yet"
**Solution:** Be patient! It takes 1-3 days for first indexing, then 2-4 weeks to rank.

### "Performance score is low"
**Solution:** This is normal with 3D graphics. Follow PERFORMANCE_GUIDE.md for optimization.

### "Pages not indexed"
**Solution:** 
1. Check Coverage report in Search Console
2. Ensure pages are in sitemap.xml
3. Check robots.txt isn't blocking them

### "Mobile usability issues"
**Solution:** Test on real mobile devices and fix any issues found.
